Contextual Compression Retriever

Contextual Compression Retriever is a retrieval technique in RAG that retrieves documents first and then removes the irrelevant parts of those documents, keeping only the information relevant to the user's query.

Normal Retriever:
User Query
    ↓
Vector Retriever
    ↓
Retrieve Documents
    ↓
Send COMPLETE documents to LLM



Contextual Compression:
User Query
    ↓
Vector Retriever
    ↓
Retrieve Documents
    ↓
Compression / Filtering
    ↓
Keep only relevant information
    ↓
Send to LLM
